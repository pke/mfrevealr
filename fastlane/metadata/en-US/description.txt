Point your camera at the code on the side of a minifigure box and see right away which figure is inside. No shaking, no squeezing, no guessing.

HOW IT WORKS
• Find the small square code next to the barcode on the side of the box
• Hold your phone up to it, in the store or at home
• The figure inside appears instantly

COMPLETE YOUR SERIES
Every figure you scan is ticked off. Tap the card to see the whole series: what you already have and what is still missing. You can tick figures on or off by hand at any time.

YOUR WAY
• Three layouts: a big reveal card, a split screen with the whole series always in view, or a slim strip that leaves the camera almost free
• Light and dark mode
• English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese

SUPPORTED SERIES
• Series 25, Series 26 Space, Series 27, Series 28 Animals and Series 29
• Dungeons & Dragons
• Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse
• Shrek
• F1 Collectible Race Cars
• Mighty Machines

New series and figures are downloaded automatically and stay on your phone, so scanning works offline too. No account, no ads, no tracking.
